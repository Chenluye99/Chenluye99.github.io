Hi! My name is Chenlu Ye (叶晨璐). I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at UIUC, advised by Prof. Tong Zhang.

My research focuses on self-improving agents and autonomous research, reinforcement learning for agentic LLM post-training, and decision-making. I study how agents learn from experience and improve their skills and memory, building on my work in LLM reasoning, RLHF, and robust reinforcement learning.

I am currently a research intern at Meta MSL / FAIR, working with Sainbayar Sukhbaatar and Jason Weston. Previously, I was an applied scientist intern at Amazon and received my MPhil from HKUST and B.S. from USTC.
