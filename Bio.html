Research Experience

  • Applied Scientist Intern, Amazon (2025.05 - 2026.05).

  • Ph.D. Research, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (2024.09 - present).

    • Advisor: Prof. Tong Zhang.

    • Developed Reinforce-Ada for adaptive sampling in online RL post-training and a self-rewarding correction framework for mathematical reasoning.

    • Developed an online iterative RLHF framework, implemented it with online DPO, and extended it to general preference models.

  • MPhil Research, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2021.09 - 2024.08).

    • Advisor: Prof. Tong Zhang.

  • Visiting Research Scholar, University of California, Los Angeles (2023.08 - 2023.12).
